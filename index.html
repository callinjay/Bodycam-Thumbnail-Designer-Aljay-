02 — CHATG METHOD

MY AI-ASSISTED THUMBNAIL WORKFLOW.

I use ChatGPT to turn a video title into a strong visual concept and generation prompt, then use Gemini to explore and develop the visual direction.

  1. Identify the subject, story, emotion, conflict, curiosity, and strongest visual opportunity within the title.

  2. Use ChatGPT for:

    • Title analysis
    • Creative concepts
    • Visual hooks
    • Composition ideas
    • Creative direction
    • Prompt development

  3. Turn the concept into a detailed visual-generation prompt.

  4. Use Gemini for:

    • Visual generation
    • Exploration
    • Variations
    • Visual development

  5. Review the generated possibilities and identify the strongest visual direction.

  6. Develop the selected direction into the final thumbnail concept.
